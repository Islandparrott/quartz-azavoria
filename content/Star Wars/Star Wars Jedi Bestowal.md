# Jedi: Bestowal

## Welcome to the Fallen Order, [[Padawan]]s.
None of you remember your childhood before the Jedi, but you know you were all found together in some controversy as younglings, leading to other younglings and jedi shunning you. During your time at the temple, you had always shared a close bond, you all were indoctrinated and trained together, but after [[Order 66]] you survived together. You are a close-knit group of ~20yo survivors from the [[Jedi Order]] aboard an old freighter. It has been 5 years since the Empire has been formed, and any other jedi you have been in contact with have died, until you all receive a vision of a former master...

## Character requirements:
- You are all at the level of [[Jedi]] Padawans, trained in lightsabers and the force.
- You do not need to be actively practicing your attachment to the [[Force]] or the jedi. ie. Play any class/specialization you want.
- You each know eachothers characters extensively.
- You can not be a [[droid]] or a [[clone]].

## Crew Goals:
- Oppose the [[empire.]]
- Rebuild your [[lightsabers]].
- Find your [[Jedi Master]]s.
- Complete your training.
- Get revenge on the [[Sith]]
