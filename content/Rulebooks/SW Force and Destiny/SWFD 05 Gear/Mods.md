## Mods
- when furhter changes are wanted
- can improve on performance
- most attachments have series of mods
### Installing Mods
- selects uninstalled mod
- 100c on components/supplies
- needs a tool kit & work bench
- [[Hard 3d8]] [[MECHANICS]] check
	- if FAILed, can NOT attempt again
	- if FAILs with a [[Despair Symbol]], [[ATTACHMENT]] is destroyed
- price of additional mods = 100 * # mods after installation
### [[Lightsaber]] Mods
- [[Kyber Crystal]] mods are best ways to mod sabers
- used to find crystals from crystal planets ([[Ilum]], [[Dantooine]], [[jedha]] etc)
- extremely rare
- Acquiring a crystal is a quest rather than a transaction
