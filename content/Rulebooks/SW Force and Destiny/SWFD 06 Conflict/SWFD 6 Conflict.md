# Conflict Structure
---
- Rounds and turns
	- 1 round = ~1 minute, but not specified
1. initiative
	- make SIMPLE(0d8) COOL or VIGILANCE check
	- highest to lowest
	- if tied, more triumphs is higher
		- if still tied, player takes precedence
2. Assign slots
	- each listed slot becomes a PLAYER slot vs a GM slot
3. Participants take TURNs
	- any character on the listed slot's side can take their turn in that slot
		- each character can only act once per round
4. round ends
	- repeat rounds until end of conflict
5. Encounter ends
	- once / encounter reset
	- PCs can recover strain and help incapacitated characters
## Cinematic Combat
- strongly encouraged to describe details of setup before attacking
- tactics not necessary, focus on description and dice rolling
## Cool vs Vigilance
- INITIATIVE can be rolled with COOL or VIGILANCE depending on situation
- COOL when aware and ready for combat
- VIGILANCE when conflict is unexpected

# Turn
- can take any number of things:
	- take cover, unlock a door, shoot a blaster
- ACTIVITIES: Incidentals, maneuvers & Actions
## Incidentals
---
- little time/effort
- drop an item, talking, switching blaster mode, minor movements, peeking around corners etc
- no limit on incidentals
## 207 Maneuvers
---
- non-skill check activities
- take time & effort
- allowed 1 maneuver,
	- some circumstances allow for 2
- example: Aiming, moving one RANGE band closer, opening door, taking cover
### Limitations
- 1 free maneuver
- max 2 maneuvers
- may perform additional maneuver by taking 2 STRAIN
- can gain bonus maneuver from other actions..
	- ie. NPC failing horribly on stealth check may allow PCs to take maneuver during NPC turn
	- bonus maneuvers dont count against max
	- no limit to bonus maneuvers, but recommended max 2 total maneuvers per character per round
### Flying
- Hovering counts as moving on the ground, 
	- but ignores terrain features as long as they can stay above it
- Flying use aerodynamics. 
	- must use 1 maneuver to move
## 209 Actions
---
- skill check activities
- 1 action during turn
- always involve skill check
	- may use abilities
- ie. slicing a computer, shooting someone, unlocking a door, instructing allies with orders, performing first aid, sneaking up on enemy, climbing cliff
### Combat Check
1. declare targets
2. assemble pool
3. pool results, deal damage
4. resolve Advantage & threat
5. resolve triumph & despair
6. reduce damage, apply wound, apply injury

# Range
---
## Engaged
- right up next to it, 
- melee range/touch range
## Short
- several meters
- thrown weapons, small firearms
- 1 short-short range = maneuver to move
## Medium
- several dozen meters
- reliable pistols, few thrown weapons
- need to talk loudly to hear
- short-medium = 1 maneuver
## Long
- farther than few dozen meters
- blaster rifles, mounted weapons/gunnery
- yell loudly to hear
- medium > long = 2 maneuvers
- short > long = 3 maneuvers
## Extreme
- snipers, vehicle armaments
- may not be able to hear
- time consuming to move
- Long > extreme = 2 maneuvers
## Planetary Scale
- takes up next scale past Personal Extreme range

# 217 Additional Modifiers
---
## Ranged vs Engaged targets
- If target is engaged with another target
	- 1 difficulty
	- if generating a THREAT, spent to hit engaged target instead of chosen target
## Ranged while engaged
- when character makes ranged attack at engaged distance 
	- Character engaging gains +1 BOOST to melee attack
	- lost if no longer engaged
![[Pasted image 20260303071922.png]]
## Prone Attacking
- +1 BOOST to MELEE attacks against prone target
- +1 SETBACK to MELEE attacks while prone
- +1 SETBACK to RANGED attacks against prone target
## Dual Wielding
- can be any two weapons they can reasonably hold
	- light ranged, one-handed melee weapons
	- GM has final say
- roll COMBINED check
	- player designates 1 weapon as PRIMARY
	- when rolling, use PRIMARY weapon
	- Compare characteristics/skills used for each weapon
		- take the lowest of each and use to assemble.
	- Compare difficulty of both checks
		- take highest difficulty
		- +1 if skills are the same
		- +2 if skills are different
- If Hit
	- primary weapon hits, 
	- may spend 2 ADVANTAGE or 1 TRIUMPH to hit with secondary
	- If both hit, may spend 1 ADVANTAGE or 1 TRIUMPH to activate qualities
	- Each hit deals damage as normal
## Unarmed
---
- BRAWL check
- base damage of BRAWN
- CRIT: 5
- Disorient 1, Knockdown
- attacker can choose to damage STRAIN instead of WOUND
	- still reduced by SOAK
### Grappling
- Focus on narrative, no specific rules for grappling
- use UNARMED check to see if they succeed
## Improvised Weapons
---
- use MELEE skill
- add BRAWN to DAM
- akin to INFERIOR weapons, +1 auto THREAT
- DAM based on size/weight
- 1 DESPAIR or 2 THREAT breaks weapon, becomes useless
- large weapons require 2 hands for Silhouette 1 or lesser

---
- if target is 2 SILHOUETTEs larger than attacker, -1 DIFFICULTY to attack
- if target is 2 SILHOUETTEs smaller, +1 DIFFICULTY

# Wounds
## Wound Threshold
--
- each DAM causes 1 WOUND
- When taking a WOUND > WOUND THRESHOLD,
	- become incapacitated
 	- until reduced to at or below WT
	- NPCs  are 'defeated',  not always death.
 		- Can be passed out from shock, crippled, unoncious or others
- Non-Nemesis do not track strain
	- add strain straight to Wounds
 	- Results depends on what latest wound was done by
## Strain Threshold
---
- impairs character but doesnt inflict wounds
	- hours wandering through desert, minor shock from a wire, battered around in a trash compactor, stun weapons, stress, fear, anxiety, emotional turmoil, losing cool, berations
- Accumulates slowly as environmental, or in large chunks when rattled by something
- can Voluntarily suffer strain for effects
- When taking Strain > Strain Threshold, become incapacitated & unconcious or dazed/staggered to be unaware
- NPCs take STRAIN DAM straight to WOUND
### Strain Crit Injuries
- strain can trigger crit injuries
- if NPC is incapacitated by Strain Crit Injury, they are unconcious
- is PC/Nemesis takes crit injury from stun, can be long-term effects
	- knocked off balance, take crack to skull
- may choose for these crit injuries to do nothing instead.
## States of Health
---
- Unwounded
	- no injuries/wounds
- Wounded
	- any wounds.
 	- taken cuts, bruises, scrapes
  	- no permanent damage
- Critically Injured
	- suffering from Crit Injury
 	- Actual Injuries with effect
- Incapacitated
	- more wounds than threshold
 	- Unconcious, cant act until wounds decresed below WT
  	- automatically gains Crit Injury
## Critical Injuries
---
- taken when exceeding wound threshold
- roll 1d100 on crit table
- long term injuries affect until properly medically treated
- Critical Injury status remains even if Short-Term effects have worn off until treated
- Each Crit Injury adds +10 to any crit injury roll
## Death
---
- If character rolls too many crit injuries and reaches the DEATH result, they die
- No characters may be revived
	- destroyed droid cant be revived (CPU destroyed)

# Healing
## Natural Rest
---
- each Full Nights rest, heal 1 wound
- after 1 week of rest, roll RESILIENCE
	- Difficulty = Crit Injury severity rating
	- success: recover from crit injury
	- fail: no effect
 	- triumph: heal additional crit injury 
- Droids heal in the same fashion
## Medical Care
---
- May attempt MEDICINE check
	- difficulty = State of Health
 	- heals WOUNDs = successes
  	- heals STRAIN = advantages
- only 1 MEDICINE check each encounter
- may attempt to heal CRIT INJURY with MEDICINE check
	- difficulty = CRIT INJURY SEVERITY
 	- 1 medicine check / week / crit injury
- can attempt to MEDICINE check own wounds
	- +2 difficulty
 	- +1 if droid
- without equipment, +1 difficulty
- Droids use MECHANICS
## Bacta Tanks
---
- heal 1 wound every 2 hours
- incapacitated heals 1 wound every 6 hours
- after each day, attempt RESILIENCE check
	- heal crit injury
### Oil Baths
- each hour, heal 1 wound
- do not remove crit injuries
## Stimpacks
---
- no roll required
- heals 5 wounds
- wounds healed lessens by 1 each use.  must wait 24 hours before being used normally
	- 6th stimpack would have no effect
- takes 1 maneuver
- must be engaged
- no effect on crit injuries
### Repair Patches
- heal 3 wounds
- does not lessen
## Strain Recovery
---
- easy
- after encounter, SIMPLE DISCIPLINE or COOL check
	- each success = recover 1 STRAIN
 - 1 nights rest removes all strain.
## Recovering Crit Injuries
---
- should be treated quickly
- after effects expire, injury persists
