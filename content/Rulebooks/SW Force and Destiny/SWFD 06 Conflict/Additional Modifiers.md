## Ranged vs Engaged targets
- If target is engaged with another target
	- 1 difficulty
	- if generating a THREAT, spent to hit engaged target instead of chosen target
## Ranged while engaged
- when character makes ranged attack at engaged distance 
	- Character engaging gains +1 BOOST to melee attack
	- lost if no longer engaged

## Prone Attacking
- +1 BOOST to MELEE attacks against prone target
- +1 SETBACK to MELEE attacks while prone
- +1 SETBACK to RANGED attacks against prone target
## Dual Wielding
- can be any two weapons they can reasonably hold
	- light ranged, one-handed melee weapons
	- GM has final say
- roll COMBINED check
	- player designates 1 weapon as PRIMARY
	- when rolling, use PRIMARY weapon
	- Compare characteristics/skills used for each weapon
		- take the lowest of each and use to assemble.
	- Compare difficulty of both checks
		- take highest difficulty
		- +1 if skills are the same
		- +2 if skills are different
- If Hit
	- primary weapon hits, 
	- may spend 2 ADVANTAGE or 1 TRIUMPH to hit with secondary
	- If both hit, may spend 1 ADVANTAGE or 1 TRIUMPH to activate qualities
	- Each hit deals damage as normal
## [[Unarmed]]
---
- BRAWL check
- base damage of BRAWN
- CRIT: 5
- Disorient 1, Knockdown
- attacker can choose to damage STRAIN instead of WOUND
	- still reduced by SOAK
### [[Grappling]]
- Focus on narrative, no specific rules for grappling
- use UNARMED check to see if they succeed
## [[Improvised Weapons]]
---
- use MELEE skill
- add BRAWN to DAM
- akin to INFERIOR weapons, +1 auto THREAT
- DAM based on size/weight
- 1 DESPAIR or 2 THREAT breaks weapon, becomes useless
- large weapons require 2 hands for Silhouette 1 or lesser

## [[Silhouettes]]
---
- if target is 2 SILHOUETTEs larger than attacker, -1 DIFFICULTY to attack
- if target is 2 SILHOUETTEs smaller, +1 DIFFICULTY
