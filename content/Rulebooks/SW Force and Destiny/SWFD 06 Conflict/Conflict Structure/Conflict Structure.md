- [[Round]]s and [[Turn]]s
	- 1 round = ~1 minute, but not specified
1. [[initiative]]
	- make [[Simple 0d8]] [[COOL]] or [[VIGILANCE]] check
	- highest to lowest
	- if tied, more triumphs is higher
		- if still tied, player takes precedence
2. Assign slots
	- each listed slot becomes a PLAYER slot vs a GM slot
3. Participants take TURNs
	- any character on the listed slot's side can take their turn in that slot
		- each character can only act once per round
4. [[round]] ends
	- repeat rounds until end of conflict
5. [[Encounter]] ends
	- once / encounter reset
	- PCs can recover strain and help incapacitated characters
## Cinematic Combat
- strongly encouraged to describe details of setup before attacking
- tactics not necessary, focus on description and dice rolling
## [[Cool]] vs [[Vigilance]]
- [[INITIATIVE]] can be rolled with COOL or VIGILANCE depending on situation
- COOL when aware and ready for combat
- VIGILANCE when conflict is unexpected
