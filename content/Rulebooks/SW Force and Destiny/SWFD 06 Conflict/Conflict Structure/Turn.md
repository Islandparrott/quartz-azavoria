- can take any number of things:
	- take cover, unlock a door, shoot a blaster
- ACTIVITIES: Incidentals, maneuvers & Actions
## [[Incidentals]]
---
- little time/effort
- drop an item, talking, switching blaster mode, minor movements, peeking around corners etc
- no limit on incidentals
## 207 [[Maneuvers]]
---
- non-skill check activities
- take time & effort
- allowed 1 maneuver,
	- some circumstances allow for 2
- example: Aiming, moving one RANGE band closer, opening door, taking cover
### Limitations
- 1 free maneuver
- max 2 maneuvers
- may perform additional maneuver by taking 2 STRAIN
- can gain bonus maneuver from other actions..
	- ie. NPC failing horribly on stealth check may allow PCs to take maneuver during NPC turn
	- bonus maneuvers dont count against max
	- no limit to bonus maneuvers, but recommended max 2 total maneuvers per character per round
### Flying
- Hovering counts as moving on the ground, 
	- but ignores terrain features as long as they can stay above it
- Flying use aerodynamics. 
	- must use 1 maneuver to move
## 209 [[Action]]s
---
- skill check activities
- 1 action during turn
- always involve skill check
	- may use abilities
- ie. slicing a computer, shooting someone, unlocking a door, instructing allies with orders, performing first aid, sneaking up on enemy, climbing cliff
### Combat Check
1. declare targets
2. assemble pool
3. pool results, deal damage
4. resolve Advantage & threat
5. resolve triumph & despair
6. reduce damage, apply wound, apply injury
