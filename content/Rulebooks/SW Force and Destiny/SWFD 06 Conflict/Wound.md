## [[Wound Threshold]]

## [[Strain Threshold]]
---
- impairs character but doesnt inflict wounds
	- hours wandering through desert, minor shock from a wire, battered around in a trash compactor, stun weapons, stress, fear, anxiety, emotional turmoil, losing cool, berations
- Accumulates slowly as environmental, or in large chunks when rattled by something
- can Voluntarily suffer strain for effects
- When taking Strain > Strain Threshold, become incapacitated & unconcious or dazed/staggered to be unaware
- NPCs take STRAIN DAM straight to WOUND
### Strain Crit Injuries
- strain can trigger crit injuries
- if NPC is incapacitated by Strain Crit Injury, they are unconcious
- is PC/Nemesis takes crit injury from stun, can be long-term effects
	- knocked off balance, take crack to skull
- may choose for these crit injuries to do nothing instead.
## [[States of Health]]
---
- Unwounded
	- no injuries/wounds
- Wounded
	- any wounds.
 	- taken cuts, bruises, scrapes
  	- no permanent damage
- Critically Injured
	- suffering from Crit Injury
 	- Actual Injuries with effect
- Incapacitated
	- more wounds than threshold
 	- Unconcious, cant act until wounds decresed below WT
  	- automatically gains Crit Injury
## [[Critical Injuries]]
---
- taken when exceeding wound threshold
- roll 1d100 on crit table
- long term injuries affect until properly medically treated
- Critical Injury status remains even if Short-Term effects have worn off until treated
- Each Crit Injury adds +10 to any crit injury roll
## [[Death]]
---
- If character rolls too many crit injuries and reaches the DEATH result, they die
- No characters may be revived
	- destroyed droid cant be revived (CPU destroyed)
