## [[Engaged Range]]
- right up next to it, 
- melee range/touch range
## [[Short Range]]
- several meters
- thrown weapons, small firearms
- 1 short-short range = maneuver to move
## [[Medium Range]]
- several dozen meters
- reliable pistols, few thrown weapons
- need to talk loudly to hear
- short-medium = 1 maneuver
## [[Long Range]]
- farther than few dozen meters
- blaster rifles, mounted weapons/gunnery
- yell loudly to hear
- medium > long = 2 maneuvers
- short > long = 3 maneuvers
## [[Extreme Range]]
- snipers, vehicle armaments
- may not be able to hear
- time consuming to move
- Long > extreme = 2 maneuvers
## Planetary Scale
- takes up next scale past Personal Extreme range
