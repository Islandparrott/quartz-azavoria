# 31 Interpreting the Pool
---
## [[Success Symbol]] / [[Failure Symbol]]
- Each fail cancels out a success
- need at least 1 success to succeed
- triumph/despair counts as success/fail
## [[Advantage Symbol]] / [[Threat Symbol]]
- threat cancels advantage
- only affects when one outnumbers the other
- player chooses how to use advantage
	- using weapon quality, maneuver, recover strain
- Threat are by GM
	- suffer strain, provide opportunity to opponent, falling prone, environmental, taking longer
## [[Triumph Symbol]] / [[Despair Symbol]]
- does not cancel out
- both results interpret separately
- more powerful version of advantage/threat
	- advantage/threat: recover/lose strain, triumph/despair: heal/gain wound
