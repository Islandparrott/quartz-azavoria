## Starting XP
- gain XP based on [[species]]
- used during character creation as a currency
- any unspent points can be saved for later
- Can get more experience when determining starting [[Morality]] (pg49)
## [[Characteristic]] Improvement
- increasing characteristics costs 10 * next rating value
	- can only be increased- creation.
	- (3 to 4 = 40XP)
- can improve later through talents
- max 5 in creation
- total max of 6 per stat
- Each improvement must be sequential
## [[Skill]] improvement
- skills have 5 ranks of training
	- max of rank 2 during creation
- [[career]]/[[specialization]] gives some ranks for free
- must be improved sequentially
	- Career skill cost = next rank level * 5 (1 to 2 = 10xp)
	- non-career skill cost =  5 + (next rank level * 5) (1 to 2 = 15xp)
## Acquiring [[Talents]]
- each [[specialization]] [[talent tree]] has 4 columns, 5 rows
- top row is cheapest (5xp), bottom is most expensive (25xp)
- all first row is available for purchase
- must follow line of purchases to acquire the rest.
- each selection may be purchased once
- the same talent may appear in multiple places, this will cause effects to stack
- if multi-specialization and already purchased a talent, PC may need to purchase again.
	- if ranked, PC will need to purchase again & will gain another rank
 	- if unranked: counts as already purchased, may acquire with no cost and no additional effects.
## Acquiring [[Specialization]]s
- Each character starts with 1 specialization
- may purchase as many additional specializations as they want
- gains benefits of new specializations career skills
- Can purchase specializations from any [[career]]
	- price of career specializations = 10 * total # of specializations after purchase
	- Non-career specialization price = 10 * # of specializations after purchase + 10
## Buying [[force power]]
- [[force power]] are different from [[force talent]] (pg282)
- PCs start without force powers, may purchase if wished.

## Knight-Level play (pg322)
- GM may choose to start at higher level
- players immediately take larger role
- similar power to a young [[jedi knight]]
- +150 XP
- max skill rank of 3
- Gain a Basic Lightsaber (pg176) or 9000 credits of gear/vehicle
