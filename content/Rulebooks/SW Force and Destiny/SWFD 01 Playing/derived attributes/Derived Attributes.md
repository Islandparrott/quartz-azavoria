## [[Wound Threshold]]
- how much damage before knocked out
- Wounds can lead to critical injury
- treated with Medicine, take time to recover (droids use mechanics)
- starting WT is based on Brawn on character creation
- can not be increased by increasing Brawn.
- can only be increased by purchasing talents
## [[Strain Threshold]]
- How much stress before stunned, dazed, incapacitated
- psyche/mental damage
- more easily suffered/treated than wounds
- based on Willpower
- increasing after creation does not increase ST
- only increased through talents
## [[Defense]]
- difficulty to hit
- characters have Ranged defense & melee defense
	- if not distinguished, defense is equal
- default defense = 0
- gained by armor, cover, talents
## [[Soak]] Value
- How much damage they can shrug off before taking real damage
- SV is subtracted from damage, remaining damage applies wounds to character
- default SV = Brawn
- Increasing brawn increases SV
- gained by armor, talents & increasing brawn
