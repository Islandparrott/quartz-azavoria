# Sessions
## First session
- recommended to run adventure highly recommended
- give quick RP and encounter
- plan 1 combat, 1 roleplay and opportunity for force powers
- later, add space combat and special rules
## Continuing story
- discuss if players had fun, how next session can improve
- award [[experience]] (p320) and credits/equipment
- give ability to change characters as will
- Story creation (p330)
## Star Wars Feeling
- make game feel like movie/tv scene
- fast pace,d entertaining, humorous and high drama/tension
- limit Horror, gruesome scenes
## Splitting up
- splitting up is fine
- dont let too much downtime pass
- Acceptable and expected
- longer term splits work but should be careful
## Absent Players
- find reason for character to be off-screen.
- other PCs play character
  - if holding unknown info, GM can run,
- if PC is critical, best to postpone
  - last resort

# Running the Game
## Interpreting [[Dice Pool]]
- players can translate into narrative effects
- rolls should not be rapidly asessed.
- Proficiency success may mean different than Ability or boost
  - proficiency means skill overcame challenge
  - boost means fortune etc
- Dice
  - [[boost d6]] & [[setback d6]]: fortune/misfortune.  Terrain, situation or task
  - [[Ability d8]] and [[Difficulty d8]]: abilities vs difficulty
  - [[Proficiency d12]] and [[? d12]]: skills and more difficulty
- Symbols
- [[Advantage]] [[Disadvantage]]: may trigger talents/abilities/effects.  gives GM opportunity how result influences position
- [[Triumph]] and [[despair]]: generate excitement.  Critical success/failure
- all may be spent on narrative effect instead of mechanical effect
## [[Boost d6]] & [[Setback d6]]
p316
- allows PCs to try unusual/insane ideas during play
- encourage creative solutions.
- instead of saying NO, add more setback
- boost can reward for good planning/thinking
- used in areas not covered by rules
## Using [[Strain]]
p317
- [[strain]] (p222) used for narrative-mechanical emphasis/consequences
- allow voluntary strain for maneuvers
- strain emulates environmental damage
- extreme stress/psychological pressure
## Death
- unlikely in most sessions
## Feedback  &Improvement
- ask for feedback every few sessions
- especially during difficult/dull games

# Awarding [[Expperience]]
p320
- give every session
- Advancement brackets:
  - Slow advancement: 10-15XP per PC
  - Moderate advancement: 20XP
  - Quick: 25XP
- Average: 10-20XP for 2-3 encounters ( see above)
- +5XP for key milestone/story arc
- +1-2XP for RP/clever thinking
- GM should give source of XP:
  - +5XP for avoiding Bounty Hunter
  - +5XP for transporting cargo

# Game Master Rules and Options
## using [[Morality]]
- Being good vs power
- PCs considered "good guys" until Morality drops below 30
- PC may choose to start as Dark Side
## Triggering Morality
![[Triggering morality]]
- make list of each PCs morality value
- roll 1d100 at start of session, find player with closest score.
  - that player has morality triggered.
- Make an effort to engage that PCs [[emotional strength]] and [[emotional weakness]]
- Player gains chance to do something particularly good or evil.
- If engaging succesfully, double morality increase/decrease
