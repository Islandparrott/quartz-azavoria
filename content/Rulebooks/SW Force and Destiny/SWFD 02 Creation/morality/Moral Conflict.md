### Morality & Conflict
- PC tracks morality
- can become Paragon of the [[Light Side]] or fall the the [[Dark Side]]
- [[Morality]] has chance of changing at end of session
	- actions affect probability
	- whether or not increases determined through [[Moral Conflict]]

## [[Moral Conflict]]
- starts at 0 moral conflict each session
- resource accumulated based on choices and actions
- more conflict =  chance of decreasing morality
- [[Moral Conflict]] Earned by
	- using [[dark side]] to generate [[force point]] when activating [[force power]] or [[force talent]].
		- +1 [[Moral Conflict]] per [[Dark Side]] result
	- immoral actions generates significant amount of [[Moral Conflict]]
		- Extreme actions, taking lives.
		- GM should inform player that action will cause earning [[Moral Conflict]].  doesnt need to tell exact amount
	- generating results on failing a [[fear check]]
		- GM can have PC suffer [[Moral Conflict]] = [[Difficulty]] instead of normal penalties
		- generating [[Despair Symbol]] on [[fear check]] may cause automatically
- at most 10 conflict in 1 session (more if GM declares it)
- at end of session, roll 1d10.  
	- if roll < [[Moral Conflict]], subtract result from [[Moral Conflict]] & [[Morality]]
	- if roll > [[Moral Conflict]], subtract [[Moral Conflict]] from result and add to [[Morality]]
	- if same, nothing happens
