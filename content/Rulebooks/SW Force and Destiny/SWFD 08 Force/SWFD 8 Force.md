
# The [[Force]]
- most NPCs have [[Force Rating]] of 0
- all PCs in [[Force and Destiny]] start with force rating of 1
## [[Force D12]]
![[Force D12]]

## Force-Sensitive Characters
- force sensitive at [[Force Rating]] >= 1
## Becoming [[Force sensitive]]
- [[Force and Destiny]] [[Career]]s have [[Force Rating]] of 1
- Careers w/o a force rating must select a force-sensitive [[Specialization]]
- Force abilities work in 2 ways
	1. PC makes [[Force Power Check]]
		- rolling [[Force D12]] to generate [[force point]]s.  
		- can spend force points to use abilities in the ability
	2. PC can COMMIT force dice.  
		- while force dice are committed to a power
		- PC counts Force Rating as -1 per force die.
		- can be committed for multiple rounds, provide with ongoing effect
