- most abilities provide temp effect
- generate [[force point]]s by making[[Force Power Check]]  with [[Force D12]]
- when uses [[Force Ability]], create [[Dice Pool]]
	- number of force die added to pool cant be > [[Force Rating]]
	- each [[Light Side]] result generates 1 [[force point]] to fuel abilities
	- each [[Dark Side]] result generates no [[force point]] and disregarded
- PC may use [[Dark Side]] point to generate 1 [[force point]] as well
	- must flip a [[Destiny Point]] from light to dark
	- suffer strain = dark side results
	- suffer conflict = dark side results
- may always choose to not generate force points
- after [[Force Power Check]], action resolved.  always 'successful'
### Combined Force Power Checks
- when combining [[Force Power Check]] with regular check, combine [[Force D12]] with normal dice pool
- may occur when dealing with powerful characters (resisting force power checks pg 283)
- character must gen enough [[force point]]s to activate ability AND succeed regular check (pg 31 for success)
### Spending [[force point]] before resolving check
- ability may allow spending point before check results
- could add a success to a roll
- may need to gen enough points to move object before check to hit
## Resisting Force Power Check
p282
- When using Force Power against PC or powerful NPC, becomes [[Opposed Check]]
- attacker makes [[dice pool]] combining [[force dice]] and [[ability]] [[proficiency]]
- must get at least 1 success
- [[skill]] used depend on attacker/defender action
